SMART STAY HUB

An IoT-based Smart PG Hostel Management System combining RFID attendance, PIR-based tailgating detection, environmental and safety monitoring, and remote appliance control — built on ESP32, MQTT (HiveMQ), and a live web dashboard.

Problem: Hostel management is manual.
Solution: Digital attendance · Visitor management · Maintenance requests · Fee reminders · Room allocation.
Startup model: Monthly subscription per hostel.

Sensors & Modules

💳
RFID RC522Resident attendance & entry authorisation
👁
PIR HC-SR501Counts persons after a tap — 1 = OK, 2 = tailgating → buzzer. Also drives energy saving
🌡
DHT22Room temperature & humidity, damp alerts
🔥
MQ-2Gas / smoke detection, fire safety
🛢
HC-SR04Overhead tank water level, “tank empty” alert
🧲
Reed SwitchMain gate / room door open-close
🔔
BuzzerLocal alarm for tailgating, gas & door-left-open
📡
ESP32Wi-Fi + MQTT bridge to this dashboard
